In our continuous effort to impart student centered education, we adhere to the SAMS core values called ETHICS: Empathy, Teamwork, Humanity, Integrity and Courage. From one room to two campuses

Our story

Follow how SAMS grew, year by year.

2012

Old Lady Piazza

Kate Adams was concerned by the number of children in her community not receiving formal education. A trained nurse and lifelong educator, she was moved to action and set up a one-room study class, affectionately nicknamed ‘Old Lady Piazza’ school, where local children could receive a meal and a few hours of primary education a day. The need was overwhelming, and the study class quickly outgrew the single room.

2013

Building the first campus

Kate Adams and her husband, Dr. Z’Sherman Adams Jr., began construction on the first SAMS campus to serve the primary education needs of the community they love and call home.

2014

SAMS opens its doors

The school opened and went on to endure the West African Ebola outbreak.

2019

Standing strong

SAMS endured the COVID-19 pandemic of 2019 and stood strong.

2020

A second campus

SAMS expanded, establishing a second campus to serve junior and senior high school students.

Honoring a legacy

Who is Sherman Adams Sr.?

The late Sherman Adams Sr. was the father of Dr. Z’Sherman Adams Jr., a single parent of 10 children, a Selma, Alabama native, and a United States Army veteran. Sherman Sr. believed in education of both the mind and soul and was able to nurture his children based upon this belief when their mother passed away in a fatal accident. In his memory, honor, and legacy, SAMS bears his name.
